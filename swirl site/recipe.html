mochi recipe

ingredients:

• 1 cup glutinous sweet rice flour (mochiko) • 1/4 cup granulated sugar • 3/4 cup water • 1/4 to 1/2 cup cornstarch or potato starch (for dusting)

instructions:

1. Mix the batter: Whisk the glutinous rice flour, sugar, and water together until smooth. 2. Microwave: Cover the bowl and microwave on high for 2 minutes. 3. Stir and finish cooking: Stir the mixture well, re-cover, and microwave for another 1 to 2 minutes. 4. Prep starch surface: Generously dust a cutting board or tray and your hands with cornstarch or potato starch. 5. Shape: Transfer the warm mochi onto the starch-dusted surface. Let it cool then cut into squares or pinch off portions, flatten gently, add filling if desired, and seal into balls. 6. Coat and store
